TF-Tracker - Privacy Policy

TF-Tracker ("Tracking of Transformer") is a web app that helps electricity distribution offices track transformer complaints, PRs, issue, replacement, repair and testing. Last updated: 10 November 2026.

Who uses the app and how they log in

Only staff of the department (Admins, JEs, M&R and other users created by an Admin) log in. Each person gets a User ID and a password from their Admin. The app does not ask for a Google or any other social account, and does not ask for your personal email address. Passwords are handled by the login service (Supabase Authentication); they are stored only in a protected (hashed) form and are never visible to anyone, including the Admin.

What data is stored

  • Work records: complaint numbers, PR numbers, places of damage, capacity, firm, workshop and division, dates of PR, issue, replacement and return, DTC, kitty, repair, scrap and testing-register entries, and remarks.
  • People: User ID, display name, role, division / workshops, tab access, and the names and mobile numbers of JEs and drivers that the offices enter for their work.
  • Technical: the date and time of changes and the User ID that made them, so that the Admin can see who entered what.

The data is kept in a database hosted by Supabase. Access is controlled by database rules (row level security): every login sees only the data of its own workshops, division or DISCOM, as set by the Admin. Records older than the retention period set by the department (about 2 years and 2 months) are deleted automatically.

Complaint status page

Anyone can check the status of a complaint on the home page by typing a complaint number, PR number or place. The result shows the complaint details stored by the offices, which can include the name and mobile number of the JE and driver.

Optional Google Sheet (SIR)

An Admin may link a Google Sheet (the SIR form sheet) to see which PRs have an SIR. If the sheet is public ("Anyone with the link" or published to the web), your browser reads it directly from Google to match PR numbers. Nothing is written to that sheet by the app.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps small preferences and a saved copy of the last answers in your browser (local storage) so that screens open quickly, and a cache of the app files so that it works as an installed app. The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties, except the services needed to run the app: Supabase (database and login) and GitHub Pages (hosting of the web pages).

Your choices

  • To correct or remove your data, or to change or remove your User ID, ask your Admin. An Admin can reset a password or delete a user at any time.
  • You can clear the data stored on your device by clearing the site data of this app in your browser, or by logging out.
